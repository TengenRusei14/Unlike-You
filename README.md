# Unlike-You

## A toxic yuri visual novel about love, obsession, and the faces we hide.
Toxic Yuri Psychological Drama 
3 Perspectives on Identity & Gender

## Story

Mina has loved Jose for fifteen years  since the day they met as children. She protected her, followed her, and scared away anyone who tried to get close.

But Jose was never hers.

When Jose falls for Ruby, Mina's world shatters. Obsession curdles into something darker, and one night in the park, everything ends in blood.

Now Mina sits in a detention center, her hands stained in blood, as Professor Tengen Ruise tries to unravel the truth. But whose truth is it?

Three perspectives. 
One tragedy. 
And a choice that changes everything.

## Features
- Three Routes
- Play as Mina, Jose, or Ruby — each reveals a different side of the story.
- Dark Romance
- A psychological thriller wrapped in obsessive love and toxic devotion.
- Multiple Endings
- Your choices shape the fate of every character.
- Original Art
- Hand-drawn character sprites and atmospheric backgrounds.
